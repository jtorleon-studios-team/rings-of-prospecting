## Intro :

- 📦 **This mod requires "Curios" for versions >=1.16.5** !
- 📦 **This mod requires "Baubles" for version 1.12.2** !

This mod adds a series of rings to help you explore the world in search of ores like diamonds, iron, gold, and more. The rings are crafted from corresponding **prospecting sticks**, such as a **diamond stick** for a diamond ring. 

### Features:
- Prospecting rings and sticks for the following ores:
  - **Iron** (Iron Prospecting Stick > Iron Prospecting Ring 💍)
  - **Coal** (Coal Prospecting Stick > Coal Prospecting Ring 💍)
  - **Gold** (Gold Prospecting Stick > Gold Prospecting Ring 💍)
  - **Diamond** (Diamond Prospecting Stick > Diamond Prospecting Ring 💍)
  - **Lapis** (Lapis Prospecting Stick > Lapis Prospecting Ring 💍)
  - **Emerald** (Emerald Prospecting Stick > Emerald Prospecting Ring 💍)
  - **Copper** (Copper Prospecting Stick > Copper Prospecting Ring 💍) (version > 1.17.1)
  - **Amethyst** (Amethyst Prospecting Stick > Amethyst Prospecting Ring 💍) (version > 1.17.1)
  - **Nether Gold** (Nether Gold Prospecting Stick > Nether Gold Prospecting Ring 💍)
  - **Nether Quartz** (Nether Quartz Prospecting Stick > Nether Quartz Prospecting Ring 💍)
  - **Nether Debris** (Nether Debris Prospecting Stick > Nether Debris Prospecting Ring 💍)

- To use, right-click with the **prospecting stick** to activate the ability. For the **prospecting ring**, equip it in the **Curios/Baubles slot** and the ability will be automatically activated.
- The rings will prospect vertically along the Y-axis, from bedrock to your current player position.
- Once the corresponding ore is detected, a musical note will play to alert you.
